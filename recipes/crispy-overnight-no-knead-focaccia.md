## 🥣 Ingredients
- [ ] 500 g bread flour (approx. 4 cups)
- [ ] 2 tsp kosher salt (6 g)
- [ ] 7 g instant yeast (2 1/4 tsp / 1 standard packet)
- [ ] 350 g warm water (approx. 1 1/2 cups)
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil (30 ml)

## 🥣 Ingredients
- [ ] Generous olive oil (for coating 9x13 pan and drizzling top)
- [ ] Flaky sea salt (Maldon or coarse salt, to taste)
- [ ] Optional toppings of choice (fresh rosemary, garlic, cherry tomatoes, or olives)

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine 500 g bread flour, 2 tsp kosher salt, 7 g instant yeast, 350 g warm water, and 2 tbsp olive oil.
2. Mix thoroughly with a spatula or wooden spoon until completely combined and a wet, sticky dough forms.
3. Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap and place in the refrigerator to cold ferment for 12 to 24 hours until doubled in size and visibly bubbly.
4. The next day, preheat your oven to 450°F (232°C). Coat a 9x13-inch metal baking pan generously with olive oil along the bottom and sides to prevent sticking.
5. Gently pour the chilled dough into the prepared pan and ease it outward toward the edges and corners until it mostly covers the bottom.
6. Cover loosely and let proof at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours until puffed and billowy.
7. Lightly wet your fingers with water to prevent sticking, then press firmly straight down into the dough with your thumbs, pointer, and middle fingers to create deep dimples throughout.
8. Generously drizzle olive oil over the surface, ensuring oil pools into the dimples, and finish with a liberal sprinkle of flaky sea salt and any desired toppings.
9. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the top and bottom are deep golden brown and crisp.
10. Let cool in the pan on a wire rack for 10 to 20 minutes before slicing and serving.
