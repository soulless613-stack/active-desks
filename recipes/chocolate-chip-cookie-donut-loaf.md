# Chocolate Chip Cookie Donut Loaf

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Angela Heckler (@obsessedwithcashews)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcbhSQLNA4N/) | [Obsessed Cakes](https://obsessedcakes.com/recipes/chocolate-chip-cookie-donut-loaf)

**Yield**: 10 Slices (9x5" Loaf Pan)

## 🍪 Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Streusel
- [ ] 60g (1/2 cup) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 32g (2 tbsp + 1 3/4 tsp) granulated sugar
- [ ] 16g (1 tbsp + 1/2 tsp) light brown sugar
- [ ] 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- [ ] 45g (3 tbsp + 1/2 tsp) cold unsalted butter, cubed
- [ ] 1/4 tsp vanilla bean paste (or pure vanilla extract)
- [ ] 48g (1/4 cup) mini semi-sweet chocolate chips
- [ ] 1 tbsp coarse sparkling/turbinado sugar (optional, for crunchy top)

## 🍞 Donut Loaf Batter
- [ ] 208g (1 3/4 cups) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 12g (1 tbsp + 2 1/4 tsp) cornstarch
- [ ] 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 1/4 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 3/4 tsp fine sea salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly ground nutmeg (key donut bakery flavor)
- [ ] 175g (7/8 cup) granulated sugar
- [ ] 96g (3/8 cup) neutral vegetable or canola oil
- [ ] 160g (2/3 cup) plain unsweetened yogurt (or sour cream)
- [ ] 108g (1/2 cup) whole milk (room temperature)
- [ ] 2 1/2 tsp vanilla bean paste (or pure vanilla extract)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 100g–120g (5/8 to 2/3 cup) mini semi-sweet chocolate chips

## 🍯 Crackly Vanilla Glaze & Finishing
- [ ] 250g (2 cups) powdered confectioners' sugar, sifted
- [ ] 60g (1/4 cup) whole milk (warmed slightly)
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla bean paste (or pure vanilla extract)
- [ ] Pinch of fine sea salt
- [ ] Flaky sea salt (Maldon), for finishing

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Prep Pan & Oven
1. Position oven rack in the center and preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Grease a 9x5-inch metal loaf pan and line with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on the long sides for easy lifting.

### 2. Make the Streusel
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the flour, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and salt.
2. Add cold cubed butter and vanilla bean paste. Cut in using a pastry cutter, fork, or fingers until clumpy, pea-sized crumbs form.
3. Stir in the mini chocolate chips. Chill in the refrigerator while making the batter.

### 3. Mix the Batter
1. **Wet ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together the granulated sugar, neutral oil, plain yogurt, milk, vanilla bean paste, and apple cider vinegar until completely emulsified and smooth.
2. **Dry ingredients**: Sift in the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, fine salt, and ground nutmeg.
3. Whisk gently just until no dry pockets remain—do not overmix.
4. Fold in the 100g–120g mini chocolate chips with a spatula.

### 4. Assemble & Bake
1. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top with a spatula.
2. Scatter the chilled chocolate chip streusel evenly over the surface, pressing lightly so it adheres.
3. Sprinkle with coarse sanding/turbinado sugar if desired.
4. Bake at **350°F (175°C) for 50–65 minutes** (or up to 75–80 mins depending on pan material). At 45 minutes, tent loosely with aluminum foil to prevent the streusel from over-browning.
5. Loaf is done when a wooden skewer inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs (internal temp ~205°F / 96°C).

### 5. Glaze & Finish
1. Immediately sprinkle flaky Maldon sea salt over the hot streusel.
2. Let the loaf cool in the pan for 25–30 minutes, then use parchment overhangs to transfer to a wire cooling rack.
3. Whisk powdered sugar, warm milk, vanilla, and a pinch of salt until smooth and pourable.
4. Spoon or drizzle glaze generously over the warm loaf, letting it drip down the sides.
5. Allow glaze to set into a crackly crust. Cool completely (optionally chill 30 minutes in the fridge) for neat, bakery-style slices!
