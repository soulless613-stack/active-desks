# Viral Korean Salt Bread (Sogeum-ppang)

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Erin's Cozy Kitchen (@erins_cozykitchen)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQRzg3MDnma/?vrfl=M25meXIxYjk2dzY4)  
**Tags**: #baking #korean #bread #saltbread #pastry  
**Yield**: 12 Rolls (Bake Time: 12–15 mins)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Roll)
- **Calories**: 245 kcal
- **Protein**: 5g
- **Carbs**: 32g
- **Fat**: 11g

---

## 🥣 Bread Dough
- [ ] 400g (3 1/3 cups) bread flour
- [ ] 120g (1 cup) cake flour
- [ ] 365ml (1 1/2 cups + 1 tbsp) cold water
- [ ] 25g (2 tbsp) granulated sugar
- [ ] 10g (1 3/4 tsp) fine sea salt
- [ ] 25g (1/4 cup) nonfat dry milk powder
- [ ] 40g (3 tbsp) cold unsalted butter
- [ ] 7g (2 1/4 tsp) instant yeast

## 🧈 Butter Core & Topping
- [ ] 12 pieces cold salted butter (about 14g / 1 tbsp each)
- [ ] 1 tbsp coarse flaky sea salt (Maldon), for sprinkling

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Dough Preparation & First Rise
1. In a large bowl, whisk together bread flour, cake flour, sugar, salt, milk powder, and instant yeast.
2. Pour in cold water and mix until a shaggy dough forms with no dry flour remaining.
3. Add the cold unsalted butter and knead vigorously (by hand or stand mixer with dough hook) for 8–10 minutes until smooth, glossy, and elastic (passes the windowpane test).
4. Shape dough into a round ball, place in a lightly greased bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled in volume (approx. 60 minutes).

### 2. Portioning & Cone Shaping
1. Punch down dough gently and divide into 12 equal portions (~78g each).
2. Roll each portion into a smooth ball, cover with a damp cloth, and rest for 15 minutes to relax gluten.
3. Roll each ball with your palms into a tapered carrot/cone shape (~6 inches long).
4. Using a rolling pin, roll from the wide base down to the narrow tip into a long triangular strip (~12 inches long).

### 3. Butter Rolling & Final Proof
1. Place one cold salted butter baton horizontally across the wide top end of the dough strip.
2. Roll the dough up tightly toward the narrow tip, pressing the edges gently to seal in the butter, forming a crescent-like roll with visible layers.
3. Place rolls seam-side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet, spaced 2 inches apart.
4. Cover loosely and proof in a warm place until noticeably puffy and airy (approx. 45–60 minutes).

### 4. High-Heat Bake & Butter Fry
1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
2. Lightly mist the risen rolls with cold water spray and generously sprinkle coarse flaky sea salt over the tops.
3. Immediately reduce oven temperature to 400°F (200°C) as you slide the baking sheet into the oven.
4. Bake for 12–15 minutes until tops are deeply golden brown. (The salted butter inside will melt and sizzle into pools on the baking sheet, frying the bottom of each roll to an incredible crispy crunch).
5. Transfer to a wire rack to rest for 5 minutes. Serve warm to enjoy the crispy bottom, fluffy crumb, and buttery hollow center!
