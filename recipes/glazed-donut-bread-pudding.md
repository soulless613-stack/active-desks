# Glazed Donut Bread Pudding

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by 12 Tomatoes (@12_tomatoes)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdRs-Ofj5uI/) | [12 Tomatoes Recipe](https://12tomatoes.com/viral-donut-bread-pudding/)  
**Tags**: `#dessert` `#breadpudding` `#donuts` `#comfortfood` `#dumpandbake`  
**Yield**: 12 Servings (9x13" Baking Dish)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 455 kcal
- **Protein**: 7g
- **Carbs**: 65g
- **Fat**: 19g

---

## 🍩 Donut Base & Sweet Milk Soak
- [ ] 24 glazed or old-fashioned donut holes
- [ ] 1 can (12 oz / 354ml) evaporated milk
- [ ] 1 can (14 oz / 396g) sweetened condensed milk

## 🍰 Topping & Spices
- [ ] 1 box (13.25 oz / 375g) French vanilla cake mix (dry)
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1/2 cup (1 stick / 115g) unsalted butter, cubed into small pats
- [ ] Optional: Vanilla ice cream or whipped cream (for serving)

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Prep Pan & Oven
1. Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch metal or glass baking dish with non-stick spray or butter.

### 2. Layer Donuts & Milks
1. Arrange all 24 donut holes in an even, single layer across the bottom of the prepared dish.
2. Pour the can of evaporated milk evenly over the top of the donut holes.
3. Pour the can of sweetened condensed milk evenly across the dish, coating the donuts thoroughly.

### 3. Add Dry Cake Mix, Spice & Butter
1. Sprinkle the dry French vanilla cake mix evenly over the entire dish to cover the donut holes.
2. Dust the 2 teaspoons of ground cinnamon evenly over the cake mix layer.
3. Distribute the cubed butter pats evenly across the surface so it melts over the dry cake mix while baking.

### 4. Bake & Serve
1. Bake at **350°F (175°C) for 35–45 minutes** until the top is golden brown, bubbling around the edges, and set in the center.
2. Remove from oven and let rest for **10–15 minutes** to allow the custard soak to settle.
3. Slice into 12 squares and serve warm—optionally topped with a scoop of vanilla bean ice cream!
