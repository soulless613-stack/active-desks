# Butter Chicken Mac N’ Cheese

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Tom Walsh (@stealth_health_life)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da_Eq6axAzl/)  
**Tags**: `#mealprep` `#highprotein` `#slowcooker` `#chicken` `#pasta`  
**Yield**: 10 Servings  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 545 kcal
- **Protein**: 42g
- **Carbs**: 58g
- **Fat**: 17g

---

## 🍗 Slow Cooker Chicken Base
- [ ] 900g (32 oz) chicken breast, diced
- [ ] 2 yellow onions, diced & sautéed until browned
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) butter
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) tomato paste
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) garlic paste
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) ginger paste
- [ ] 400g (14 oz) fire-roasted tomatoes (blended smooth)
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) sugar or zero-cal sweetener

## 🌶️ Spice Blend
- [ ] 2 tbsp paprika
- [ ] 1 tbsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp spicy curry powder
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tbsp salt
- [ ] Dash of black pepper

## 🧀 Pasta & Creamy Cheese Sauce
- [ ] 672g shell pasta (cook to 50% of box time; will finish in slow cooker)
- [ ] 1 cup reserved starchy pasta water
- [ ] 400g plain 0% Greek yogurt (FAGE brand recommended)
- [ ] 120g 1/3-fat cream cheese (Neufchâtel)
- [ ] 227g (8 oz) extra sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- [ ] 80g reduced-fat mozzarella cheese, shredded
- [ ] 1 tsp garam masala
- [ ] Fresh cilantro & green onions, chopped (for garnish)

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Slow Cooker Base
1. Dice and sauté the onions in a pan until browned, then add to your slow cooker.
2. Add diced chicken breast, butter, tomato paste, garlic paste, ginger paste, blended fire-roasted tomatoes, and sweetener.
3. Mix in the spice blend (paprika, chili powder, curry powder, cumin, salt, pepper) until thoroughly coated.
4. Cover and cook on **HIGH for 2–3 hours** or **LOW for 3–4 hours** until chicken is tender.

### 2. Cook Pasta
1. Boil shell pasta in salted water for only **50% of the package time** (par-boiled so it doesn't get mushy).
2. Reserve 1 cup of starchy pasta water before draining.

### 3. Combine & Melt
1. Shred or break up the cooked chicken directly in the slow cooker sauce.
2. Pour in the reserved pasta water, Greek yogurt, cream cheese, shredded cheddar, mozzarella, and 1 tsp garam masala.
3. Stir vigorously until melted, rich, and creamy.
4. Fold in the par-cooked pasta shells and fresh cilantro/green onions.
5. Cover on low/warm for 10–15 minutes to let the pasta absorb the sauce. Divide into 10 meal prep containers!
