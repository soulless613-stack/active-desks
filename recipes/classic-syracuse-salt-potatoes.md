# Classic Syracuse Salt Potatoes

**Source**: [Home Cook (@instagram)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DJ5DABYxHug/?obrf=cWpmaHFscnB4aGhp)  
**Tags**: #potatoes #side dish #upstate new york #bbq sides #vegetarian #gluten-free  
**Yield**: 6 Servings  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 250 kcal
- **Protein**: 3g
- **Carbs**: 27g
- **Fat**: 15g

---

## 🥣 Salt Potatoes
- [ ] 907g (2 lbs) bite-sized baby potatoes (such as baby Yukon Gold or small red potatoes), unpeeled and whole
- [ ] 290g (1 cup) fine sea salt
- [ ] 1890ml (8 cups) water

## 🥣 To Serve
- [ ] 113g (1/2 cup) unsalted butter, melted
- [ ] 2g (1 tbsp) fresh chives, finely chopped

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. Rinse the baby potatoes thoroughly under cool running water, checking that their skins remain intact with no cuts or punctures so the interior stays creamy rather than overly salty.
2. In a large pot, combine the water and the fine sea salt. Stir briefly until the salt begins to dissolve.
3. Add the whole potatoes into the salted water, making sure they are fully submerged.
4. Place the pot over high heat and bring the liquid to a rolling boil.
5. Once boiling, reduce the heat slightly to medium-high to maintain a steady, active boil. Cook uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, until a fork or skewer slides easily into the center of a potato without resistance.
6. Carefully pour the potatoes into a colander to drain away all the cooking brine. Do not rinse the potatoes.
7. Return the hot, drained potatoes to the empty pot or arrange them on a wire rack for 3 to 5 minutes; as steam and residual surface moisture evaporate, a characteristic light powdery salt crust will form over the skins.
8. Transfer the hot salt potatoes to a warm serving bowl, scatter with chopped chives if desired, and serve immediately alongside small bowls of warm melted butter for generous dipping.
