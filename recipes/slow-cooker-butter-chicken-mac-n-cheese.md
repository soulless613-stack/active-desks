# Slow Cooker Butter Chicken Mac N' Cheese

**Source**: [Stealth Health Life (@stealth_health_life)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DepW0lxIqW6/?vrfl=cXB3MGwwNDZtZnJx)  
**Tags**: #high-protein #meal-prep #slow-cooker #butter-chicken #mac-and-cheese #pasta #comfort-food  
**Yield**: 10 Servings (Meal Prep)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 545 kcal
- **Protein**: 42g
- **Carbs**: 58g
- **Fat**: 17g

---

## 🥣 Chicken & Slow Cooker Base
- [ ] 900g (32 oz) chicken breast, diced
- [ ] 2 yellow onions, diced and sautéed until browned
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) butter
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) tomato paste
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) garlic paste
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) ginger paste
- [ ] 400g (14 oz) fire-roasted crushed tomatoes, blended smooth
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) zero-calorie sweetener (or granulated sugar)

## 🥣 Spice Blend
- [ ] 14g (2 tbsp) paprika
- [ ] 8g (1 tbsp) chili powder
- [ ] 6g (1 tbsp) spicy curry powder
- [ ] 5g (2 tsp) ground cumin
- [ ] 17g (1 tbsp) coarse salt
- [ ] 1g (1/4 tsp) freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 2g (1 tsp) garam masala

## 🥣 Pasta & Creamy Cheese Sauce
- [ ] 672g (24 oz) dry pasta shells, par-cooked to 50% box time
- [ ] 240ml (1 cup) reserved pasta cooking water
- [ ] 400g (14 oz) plain 0% Greek yogurt
- [ ] 120g (4.2 oz) 1/3-fat cream cheese
- [ ] 227g (8 oz) extra sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- [ ] 80g (2.8 oz) reduced-fat mozzarella cheese, shredded

## 🥣 Garnish
- [ ] 15g (1/4 cup) fresh cilantro, finely chopped
- [ ] 25g (1/4 cup) green onions, thinly sliced

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. Melt 30g butter in a skillet over medium heat and sauté the diced yellow onions until deep golden brown and caramelized.
2. Add the diced chicken breast, browned onions, tomato paste, garlic paste, ginger paste, blended fire-roasted tomatoes, and sweetener to the slow cooker insert.
3. Add the spice blend: paprika, chili powder, spicy curry powder, cumin, salt, and black pepper. Stir thoroughly until the chicken is evenly coated.
4. Cover and slow cook on High for 2 to 3 hours or on Low for 3 to 4 hours until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
5. While the chicken finishes, boil the pasta shells in salted water for exactly half the cook time indicated on the box. Drain well and reserve 1 cup of hot pasta water.
6. Stir the plain Greek yogurt and 1/3-fat cream cheese directly into the warm slow cooker base until completely melted and smooth.
7. Fold in the par-cooked pasta shells, reserved pasta water, shredded extra sharp cheddar, shredded mozzarella, and garam masala, stirring until fully incorporated and creamy.
8. Fold in the fresh chopped cilantro and sliced green onions, then divide into 10 equal portions for freezer-friendly meal prep.
