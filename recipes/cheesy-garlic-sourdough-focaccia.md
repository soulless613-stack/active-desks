# Cheesy Garlic Sourdough Focaccia

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Amy Coyne (@amybakesbread)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DE23ak3pGsq/)  
**Tags**: `#sourdough` `#focaccia` `#garlicbread` `#cheesy` `#baking`  
**Yield**: 16 Slices (9x13" Pan or 12" Skillet)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Slice)
- **Calories**: 349 kcal
- **Protein**: 14g
- **Carbs**: 33g
- **Fat**: 17g

---

## 🥖 Sourdough Focaccia Dough
- [ ] 100g active, bubbly sourdough starter (or levain)
- [ ] 425g warm water (approx. 1 3/4 cups)
- [ ] 500g bread flour (approx. 3 1/2 cups)
- [ ] 13g fine salt (approx. 2 tsp)

## 🧀 Herb Cheese Blend (Inclusions & Topping)
- [ ] 150g shredded mozzarella cheese (1 1/2 cups)
- [ ] 100g shredded sharp cheddar cheese (1 cup)
- [ ] 100g shredded Monterey Jack cheese (1 cup)
- [ ] 70g grated parmesan cheese (1/2 cup)
- [ ] 4g dried oregano (approx. 1 tbsp)
- [ ] 4g dried basil (approx. 1 tbsp)

## 🧄 Garlic Herb Butter Oil
- [ ] 55g extra virgin olive oil (1/4 cup)
- [ ] 35g unsalted butter, melted (2 1/2 tbsp)
- [ ] 3g garlic powder (1 tsp)
- [ ] 2g fine salt (1/4 tsp)
- [ ] 1g dried oregano (1 tsp)
- [ ] 1g dried basil (1 tsp)
- [ ] 2 cloves fresh garlic, finely minced (for topping)

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Mix Dough
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the active sourdough starter, warm water, salt, and bread flour with a dough whisk or spoon until a sticky, cohesive dough forms.
2. Cover and let rest for 30 minutes.

### 2. Coil Fold #1
1. Wet hands with water. Reach under the middle of the dough, gently pull upwards to stretch, and let it fold under itself.
2. Repeat for both sides and rotate the container. Perform 4–6 folds. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.

### 3. Cheese Inclusions (Coil Folds #2–4)
1. Toss shredded mozzarella, cheddar, jack, and parmesan with dried oregano and basil in a bowl.
2. **Coil Fold #2**: Sprinkle half of the cheese mixture over the dough and gently fold to laminate the cheese into the dough layers. Cover and refrigerate remaining cheese. Rest 30 minutes.
3. **Coil Folds #3 & #4**: Perform gentle coil folds at 30-minute intervals to distribute inclusions.

### 4. Bulk Fermentation
1. Cover and let the dough rest at ~78°F for 1.5–2 hours until puffy, domed, and showing scattered air bubbles around the edges (30–40% rise).

### 5. Pan Prep & First Stretch
1. Whisk olive oil, melted butter, garlic powder, salt, and dried herbs together.
2. Coat the bottom of a 9x13" metal baking pan (or 12" cast iron skillet) with half of the garlic butter oil.
3. Turn dough out into the pan and gently stretch toward the edges and corners.
4. Drizzle the remaining garlic herb oil over the top of the dough. Cover with plastic wrap.

### 6. Second Proof
1. Let the dough proof in a warm spot (78–80°F) for 3–5 hours until doubled, puffy, aerated, and jiggly.

### 7. Dimple, Top & Bake
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Scatter the 2 minced fresh garlic cloves and remaining reserved cheese mixture evenly across the top.
3. Oil your fingers and dimple the dough deeply all over from top to bottom until full of air pockets.
4. Bake at 425°F for 25–30 minutes until bubbling, golden brown, and crisp with an internal temp of 200°F.
5. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before slicing into 16 cheesy squares!
