# Homemade Cinnamon Gifflar

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Dee | Food for everyone (@mumsfoodies)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DepW0lxIqW6/?vrfl=cXB3MGwwNDZtZnJx)  
**Tags**: #cinnamonrolls #cinnamonbuns #gifflar #baking #swedish #pastry #bread  
**Yield**: 36 Mini Rolls (Bake Time: 12–15 mins)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Mini Roll)
- **Calories**: 135 kcal
- **Protein**: 2.5g
- **Carbs**: 20g
- **Fat**: 5g

---

## 🥣 Sweet Yeast Dough
- [ ] 260ml (1 cup + 1 tbsp) lukewarm milk (38–40°C / 100–104°F)
- [ ] 7g (2 1/4 tsp / 1 packet) instant yeast
- [ ] 20g (1 1/2 tbsp) granulated sugar
- [ ] 1 large egg (or substitute +50ml milk to skip egg)
- [ ] 500g (4 cups) bread flour or all-purpose flour (min 12% protein)
- [ ] 6g (1 tsp) fine salt
- [ ] 100g (7 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened

## 🧈 Cinnamon Brown Sugar Filling
- [ ] 100g (1/2 cup packed) light brown sugar
- [ ] 100g (7 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened
- [ ] 16g (2 tbsp) ground cinnamon

## 🥚 Egg Wash
- [ ] 1 egg yolk
- [ ] 5ml (1 tsp) whole milk

## 🍯 Glossy Sugar Syrup
- [ ] 60ml (1/4 cup) water
- [ ] 60g (1/4 cup + 1 tbsp) brown sugar
- [ ] 40g (3 tbsp) unsalted butter

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Dough Mixing & Bulk Rise
1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the lukewarm milk (38–40°C) and instant yeast. Add the granulated sugar and egg, whisking until smooth and frothy.
2. Add the flour, fine salt, and softened butter. Mix thoroughly with a sturdy spatula or floured hands until all dry flour is incorporated.
3. Fold the dough over itself in the bowl, rotating 90° after each fold (10–20 folds total) until glossy, supple, and lump-free. (Alternatively, turn out onto a lightly floured surface and knead by hand for 5–7 minutes).
4. Shape into a round ball, cover with a damp cloth or plastic wrap, and rest in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled in volume.

### 2. Filling & Shaping
1. In a small bowl, beat together the softened butter, brown sugar, and ground cinnamon until smooth and spreadable.
2. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
3. Turn the risen dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Roll into a large, even rectangle (approx. 12x18 inches).
4. Spread the cinnamon butter filling evenly across the entire surface, taking it right to the edges.
5. Slice the dough width-wise into 3 equal long strips. Roll each strip up tightly into a cylinder, placing them seam-side down.
6. Gently press each roll down with your palm to flatten slightly, then slice into 2-inch mini bun pieces (approx. 36 buns total).

### 3. Second Proof & Bake
1. Arrange buns on the prepared baking sheets spaced 1 inch apart. Cover loosely and let rest for 20 minutes to puff up.
2. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
3. Whisk the egg yolk and 1 tsp milk together; lightly brush over the tops of the risen buns.
4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12–15 minutes until beautifully golden brown.

### 4. Sugar Syrup Glaze
1. While the buns bake, combine the water, brown sugar, and butter in a small saucepan. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat, then lower to a gentle simmer for 3–4 minutes until syrupy.
2. The instant the buns come out of the oven piping hot, brush them generously with the hot sugar syrup.
3. Let cool for 5 minutes on the baking sheet so the glaze sets into a soft, sticky bakery crust. Serve warm!
