# Savory Pizza Cinnamon Rolls

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Lauren Ketterman (@barefoot.mimosas)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcjzA-vgND-/)  
**Tags**: `#pizza` `#baking` `#homestead` `#cinnamonrolls` `#cheesy` `#snack`  
**Yield**: 12 Rolls (12" Cast Iron Skillet or 9x13" Pan)  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Roll)
- **Calories**: 285 kcal
- **Protein**: 12g
- **Carbs**: 26g
- **Fat**: 14g

---

## 🥖 Savory Roll Dough
- [ ] 360g (3 cups) bread flour or all-purpose flour
- [ ] 7g (2 1/4 tsp / 1 packet) active dry or instant yeast
- [ ] 240ml (1 cup) warm whole milk (105°F–110°F)
- [ ] 25g (2 tbsp) olive oil or melted butter
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) sugar or honey (to feed yeast)
- [ ] 6g (1 tsp) fine sea salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp garlic powder & 1/2 tsp dried oregano (kneaded into dough for bakery flavor)

## 🍕 Pizza Filling & Inclusions
- [ ] 180g (3/4 cup) thick pizza sauce or crushed marinara
- [ ] 250g (2 1/2 cups) whole milk mozzarella cheese, shredded
- [ ] 40g (1/2 cup) grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano cheese
- [ ] 120g (approx. 40–50 slices) pepperoni or mini pepperoni, roughly chopped
- [ ] 1 tsp Italian herb seasoning (oregano, basil, thyme, rosemary)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional, for mild heat)

## 🧄 Garlic Herb Butter Glaze (Topping)
- [ ] 30g (2 tbsp) unsalted butter, melted
- [ ] 2 cloves fresh garlic, finely minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh parsley or dried Italian herbs, finely chopped
- [ ] Extra shredded mozzarella & grated parmesan for top
- [ ] Warm marinara sauce, for dipping

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Make the Dough
1. In a large bowl or stand mixer fitted with a dough hook, whisk warm milk, sugar (or honey), and yeast. Let stand 5 minutes until frothy.
2. Add olive oil, salt, garlic powder, oregano, and flour. Mix on low until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Knead for 6–8 minutes until soft, smooth, and elastic (slightly tacky but not sticky).
4. Place in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm spot for **60–75 minutes** until doubled in size.

### 2. Roll & Fill
1. Lightly flour your work surface and punch down the risen dough.
2. Roll dough out into a large **12x18-inch rectangle** about 1/4-inch thick.
3. Spread pizza sauce evenly across the dough, leaving a 1/2-inch clean border along the top edge.
4. Scatter the shredded mozzarella, grated parmesan, Italian herb seasoning, and chopped pepperoni evenly over the sauce.

### 3. Roll, Slice & Pan
1. Starting from the long edge closest to you, roll the dough tightly into an 18-inch cylinder. Pinch the top seam to seal.
2. Slice into **12 equal rounds** (about 1.5 inches wide) using unflavored dental floss, a sewing thread, or a sharp serrated knife.
3. Grease a 12-inch cast-iron skillet or a 9x13-inch baking dish with olive oil or butter.
4. Arrange rolls swirl-side up, leaving about 1/2-inch between rolls.
5. Cover loosely and let rise for **25–30 minutes** until puffy and touching.

### 4. Bake & Finish
1. Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**.
2. Whisk melted butter with minced garlic and herbs. Brush half the garlic butter over the tops of the rolls.
3. Scatter a handful of extra shredded mozzarella and a few whole pepperoni on top.
4. Bake at **375°F (190°C) for 22–28 minutes** until the edges are golden brown and the cheese in the center is bubbling and melted.
5. Brush immediately with remaining garlic herb butter. Let rest 10 minutes, pull apart, and serve warm with extra marinara for dipping!
