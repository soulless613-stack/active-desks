# 15-Minute Creamy One-Pot Garlic Herb Pasta

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Mariam (@baiti_ana)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdWrPaJA6Bb/)  
**Tags**: `#quickmeals` `#onepot` `#pasta` `#comfortfood` `#easyweeknight`  
**Yield**: 4 Servings  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 670 kcal
- **Protein**: 15g
- **Carbs**: 50g
- **Fat**: 47g

---

## 🍝 One-Pot Pasta Base
- [ ] 250g (about 3 cups) dry pasta (penne, rotini, or rigatoni)
- [ ] 360ml (1 1/2 cups) whipping cream (or heavy cream)
- [ ] 360ml (1 1/2 cups) chicken broth (or water + 1 chicken bouillon cube)
- [ ] 16g (1 tbsp) tomato paste
- [ ] 60g (1/4 cup) garlic & herbs cream cheese (or grated Parmesan)
- [ ] 28g (2 tbsp) butter (or olive oil)

## 🌿 Seasoning & Spice Blend
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1/4 tsp chili flakes (or more to taste)
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder

## 🧀 Cheesy Topping & Garnish
- [ ] 60g (1/2 cup) shredded mozzarella cheese
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. One-Pot Assembly
1. In a large skillet, saucepan, or Dutch oven, combine dry pasta, butter, tomato paste, garlic & herb cream cheese (or Parmesan), salt, black pepper, chili flakes, oregano, garlic powder, chicken broth, and whipping cream.
2. Stir gently to distribute the seasonings and ensure all dry pasta is submerged beneath the liquid.
3. Cover the pot tightly with a lid.

### 2. Simmer to Tender
1. Place the pot on the stove over medium-high heat until the liquid comes to a bubble.
2. Immediately reduce the heat to low, keep the lid on, and let it simmer for about 15 minutes (stirring once halfway through if needed) until the pasta is al dente and the starch has emulsified the cream into a rich sauce.

### 3. Melt & Garnish
1. Remove the lid and stir thoroughly to coat every noodle in the glossy sauce.
2. Scatter the shredded mozzarella cheese and fresh chopped parsley evenly over the top.
3. Replace the lid for 2–3 minutes off the heat until the mozzarella is completely melted and bubbly.
4. Serve immediately on its own or paired with sliced grilled chicken breast or a crisp green salad!
