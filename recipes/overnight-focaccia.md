## 🥣 Ingredients
- [ ] 500 g (approx. 4 cups) bread flour
- [ ] 2 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 7 g (2 1/4 tsp) instant yeast
- [ ] 350 g warm water
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil

## 🥣 Ingredients
- [ ] Olive oil (for generously coating the pan and drizzling)
- [ ] Flaky sea salt (for sprinkling)
- [ ] Optional toppings of choice

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine 500 g bread flour, 2 tsp kosher salt, 7 g instant yeast, 350 g warm water, and 2 tbsp olive oil.
2. Mix until thoroughly combined and a wet, sticky dough forms.
3. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let it proof in the refrigerator overnight for 12 to 24 hours, until doubled in size and bubbly.
4. The next day, preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C) and generously coat a 9x13-inch baking pan with olive oil all over to prevent sticking.
5. Pour the chilled dough into the pan and gently stretch it until it mostly covers the base.
6. Cover and allow the dough to proof at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours.
7. Keep your fingers slightly wet, then gently press and dimple the dough throughout using your thumb, pointer, and middle fingers.
8. Generously drizzle olive oil over the top and sprinkle with flaky salt and any desired toppings.
9. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown, then let cool for 10 to 20 minutes before serving.
