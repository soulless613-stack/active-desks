# Crispy Potato Meatballs (كرات البطاطا محشية باللحم)

**Source**: [Instagram Reel by Abir El Saghir (@abiresag)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbLqdYjI2-U/)  
**Tags**: `#dinner` `#appetizer` `#crispy` `#beef` `#potatoes`  

---

## 🥔 For the Potato Dough
- [ ] 3 boiled potatoes (mashed smooth)
- [ ] 1 cup cornstarch
- [ ] 1 tbsp salt
- [ ] 1 tbsp turmeric (for color & flavor)
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder

## 🥩 For the Spiced Meat Filling
- [ ] ½ kg (~1.1 lbs) ground beef
- [ ] 2 onions, finely chopped
- [ ] 1 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp mixed spices (Baharat / 7-spice)
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] Oil for sautéing & deep frying

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### 1. Make the Meat Filling
1. Heat a skillet with a splash of oil over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onions and cook until softened and translucent.
3. Add the ground beef, breaking it up with a wooden spoon until browned.
4. Stir in salt, paprika, mixed spices, and cinnamon.
5. Take off the heat and stir in the chopped fresh parsley. Set aside to cool slightly.

### 2. Make the Potato Dough
1. Peel and boil 3 potatoes until fork-tender.
2. Mash thoroughly while warm until completely smooth with no lumps.
3. Add the cornstarch, salt, turmeric, and garlic powder.
4. Knead into a pliable, smooth dough that holds its shape.

### 3. Assemble & Fry
1. Take a golf-ball sized portion of potato dough and flatten it into a disc in the palm of your hand.
2. Place a generous spoonful of the spiced beef filling into the center.
3. Cup your hand and pinch the edges up to completely enclose the filling, rolling gently into a smooth sphere.
4. Heat frying oil in a deep pan or pot over medium-high heat (around 350°F / 175°C).
5. Fry the potato balls in batches for 4–5 minutes until golden and deeply crispy on the outside.
6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot!
