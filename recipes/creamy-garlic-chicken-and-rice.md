# High Protein Creamy Garlic Chicken and Rice

**Source**: [Stealth Health (@stealth_health_life)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQRzg3MDnma/?vrfl=M25meXIxYjk2dzY4) | [Full Recipe Blog](https://stealthhealthrecipes.com/creamy-garlic-chicken-rice)  
**Tags**: #meal prep #high protein #chicken #one pot  
**Yield**: 10 Servings  

### 📊 Nutrition (Per Serving)
- **Calories**: 460 kcal
- **Protein**: 45g
- **Carbs**: 45g
- **Fat**: 11g

---

## 🥣 Chicken and Base
- [ ] 1360g (48 oz) chicken breast, diced
- [ ] 15ml (1 tbsp) olive oil
- [ ] 600g (3 cups) jasmine rice, dry
- [ ] 1200ml (5 cups) chicken broth
- [ ] 6 cloves garlic, minced

## 🥣 Seasonings
- [ ] 15g (1 tbsp) garlic powder
- [ ] 10g (2 tsp) onion powder
- [ ] 5g (1 tsp) smoked paprika
- [ ] 6g (1 tsp) salt
- [ ] 2g (1/2 tsp) black pepper

## 🥣 Creamy Sauce
- [ ] 224g (8 oz) light cream cheese
- [ ] 100g (1 cup) grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] 240ml (1 cup) fat-free milk
- [ ] 10g (1/4 cup) fresh parsley, chopped

---

## 🧑‍🍳 Instructions

### Preparation & Cooking
1. Dice the chicken breast into small, even bite-sized pieces.
2. In a large bowl, season the chicken with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Toss until fully coated.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear until cooked through and slightly browned (about 6-8 minutes). Remove chicken and set aside.
4. In the same skillet, add the minced garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the dry jasmine rice to the skillet and toast for 2 minutes, stirring frequently to absorb the remaining flavors in the pan.
6. Pour in the chicken broth, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15-18 minutes or until the rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
7. While the rice cooks, combine the light cream cheese, Parmesan cheese, and milk in a blender or whisk vigorously in a bowl until smooth.
8. Once the rice is done, stir in the cream sauce and the cooked chicken.
9. Mix well over low heat until the sauce is creamy and evenly distributed. Garnish with fresh parsley.
10. Divide into 10 equal portions for meal prep.
